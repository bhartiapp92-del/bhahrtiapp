Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 10, 2026 • Last Reviewed: November 2026

At भर्ती-App, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data of our job seekers, recruiters, and employers. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, store, process, and safeguard your information when you use the भर्ती-App mobile application, website, and related career matchmaking services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on your interaction with the platform, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Job Seeker Details: Full Name, Email Address, Contact Number, Resume/CV, Educational Background, Employment History, Skills, and Job Preferences.
  • Employer & Recruiter Information: Company Name, Official Work Email, Contact Details, Company Verification Documents, and Job Requisitions.
  • Account & Security Data: Login credentials, account preferences, communication timestamps, and profile completeness status.
  • Transaction Information: When you purchase or subscribe to optional features (such as Executive Premium VIP), transaction reference IDs, date, and amount are processed securely via our authorized payment gateway partners. We do not store sensitive payment credentials (such as credit card numbers or UPI PINs).

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly for legitimate operational purposes, including:

  • Enabling direct matchmaking between candidates and verified recruiters.
  • Powering one-click job applications and profile tracking.
  • Facilitating authorized communication between employers and applicants (including WhatsApp and in-app updates where chosen).
  • Verifying employer authenticity and maintaining platform integrity.
  • Managing customer support requests and billing verifications.
Our Privacy Promise: We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information to third-party data brokers or marketing agencies. Your profile information is only visible to verified recruiters and employers for genuine recruitment opportunities.

3. Data Protection and Security

We employ industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures to safeguard your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. All data transmission between your device and our servers is encrypted using Secure Socket Layer (SSL/TLS) protocols.

4. Third-Party Payment Gateway Compliance

When you activate optional paid memberships (such as the Executive Premium VIP Membership at ₹10/month), your payment is processed independently by RBI-compliant third-party Payment Aggregators (Payment Gateways). These processors adhere to the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standards (PCI-DSS) to ensure the highest degree of security for your financial transactions.

5. User Rights & Data Retention

You have the right to:

  • Access and review your personal profile details.
  • Update or correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion or deactivation of your account and personal data by reaching out to our support team.

6. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
