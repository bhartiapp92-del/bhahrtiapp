Cancellation & Refund Policy

Effective Date: November 10, 2026 • Last Reviewed: November 2026

Thank you for choosing भर्ती-App. This Cancellation and Refund Policy applies to all purchases, digital service subscriptions, and memberships conducted on the भर्ती-App platform, including the Executive Premium VIP Membership.

1. Nature of Digital Services

The core भर्ती-App platform is free to use for job search and direct application features. The Executive Premium VIP Membership (priced at ₹10 per month) is an intangible, instant-access digital product that delivers immediate premium profile tagging, enhanced recruiter visibility, and prioritized connectivity.

Digital Service Policy: Because premium access, computational resources, and recruiter routing are activated instantaneously upon transaction confirmation, subscription payments are generally non-refundable once the digital service has been successfully unlocked.

2. Cancellation Policy

  • User-Initiated Cancellation: Users can cancel their recurring monthly VIP subscription at any time prior to the next billing date through their in-app account settings.
  • Effect of Cancellation: When you cancel your subscription, auto-renewal will be immediately stopped. You will retain full access to all Executive Premium VIP features until the end of your current paid billing period. No further charges will occur thereafter.

3. Exceptions: Failed and Duplicate Transactions

We are committed to fair and transparent billing practices. Refunds are promptly reviewed and issued under the following specific circumstances:

  • Duplicate Charges: If your bank account or payment method was debited multiple times for a single VIP activation due to a network glitch or technical timeout.
  • Failed Service Delivery: If an amount was debited from your account, but VIP membership was not activated within 24 hours due to a technical failure.

4. Refund Request Procedure & Timelines

To request a review for a duplicate charge or unfulfilled transaction:

  1. Email our official support desk at brandboxai1@gmail.com from your registered email address.
  2. Include your Full Name, Registered Phone Number, Payment Gateway Transaction ID / UTR Number, and a screenshot of the payment receipt.
  3. Our billing team will verify the transaction log with the payment gateway aggregator within 24 to 48 business hours.
  4. Once approved, the refund will be initiated directly to your original payment method (Bank Account, UPI, or Card). In accordance with banking timelines, the refunded amount typically reflects in your account within 5 to 7 business days.
